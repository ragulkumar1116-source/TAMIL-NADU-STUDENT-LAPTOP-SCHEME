Representation Purpose

Supporting All Batches Who Did Not Receive Laptops

Due to the disruptions caused during the pandemic years, thousands of Government and Government-Aided school students who passed Class 12 during 2020-2021 and 2021-2022 did not receive their entitled free laptops.

This student-led portal brings together students to collect verified representations in a clean, organized, and transparent format so that structured petitions can be submitted to the School Education Department and the Chief Minister's Special Cell.

Key Verification Principles

  • Admin Verification:

  • Data Privacy Guaranteed:
    Contact numbers and personal addresses are strictly concealed from public pages.
